What ₹40 LPA really means

Your ₹40 LPA CTC includes the employer's PF and gratuity, which never reach you as cash. After removing those and deducting your own EPF (₹2,16,000), professional tax and income tax (₹6,93,395), your take-home is about ₹1,98,802/month.

You're also building ₹4,32,000/year of EPF retirement savings (employer + employee) that isn't in your monthly in-hand but is still your money.
