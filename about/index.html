About SalaryLens

SalaryLens is a free, no-sign-up tool that decodes any Indian CTC into your real monthly take-home pay. It exists because "CTC" is one of the most confusing numbers in an Indian job offer — and the gap between CTC and in-hand catches almost everyone off guard.

The web calculator shares its tax engine with the SalaryLens Chrome extension, which shows estimated in-hand salary right on job posts on LinkedIn and Naukri. Everything is computed in your browser — no accounts, no salary data sent anywhere.

Built by Adarsh Garg. Start with the in-hand salary calculator, or read the methodology to see exactly how the numbers are derived.
